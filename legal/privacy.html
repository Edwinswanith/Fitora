Privacy Policy for Apex Coaching

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Apex Coaching is a sports coaching platform for athletes, coaches, and guardians. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how users can contact us about privacy questions.

Information We Collect

We may collect account information such as name, email address, role, team or coach relationship, and login details. We may also collect athlete training and wellness information entered in the app, including training logs, attendance, RPE, sleep, hydration, mood, soreness, fatigue, recovery notes, messages, reminders, and progress updates.

When users enable notifications, we collect device notification tokens so the app can send reminders and coach messages. When users use the voice assistant, audio or transcribed text may be processed to convert spoken updates into app entries.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide coaching workflows, save user logs, show athlete progress, send reminders, support coach-athlete communication, improve app reliability, and protect the security of user accounts.

Voice Assistant and Third-Party Processing

Voice assistant features may use speech-to-text and AI processing providers to understand spoken commands and convert them into app updates. These services are used only to support app functionality.

Notifications

Apex Coaching may send push notifications for coach messages, training reminders, wellness reminders, hydration reminders, streak or activity reminders, and other app-related updates. Users can manage notification permissions from their device settings.

Sharing of Information

Information may be visible to users connected inside the app, such as an athlete's coach or guardian, depending on account role and permissions. We do not sell personal information.

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information. No internet-based service can guarantee complete security, but we work to protect account and coaching data from unauthorized access.

Children and Guardians

Apex Coaching may be used by athletes under supervision of coaches, teams, schools, clubs, or guardians. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided information without appropriate consent, they can contact us to request review or deletion.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information while an account is active or as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. Users may contact us to request account or data deletion.

Health and Coaching Disclaimer

Apex Coaching is designed for sports coaching, training logs, wellness tracking, and communication. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services.
