Delete Your Apex Coaching Account and Data

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Apex Coaching users can request deletion of their account and associated app data. This page explains how to delete your account from inside the app or request deletion by email.

Delete Your Account In The App

  1. Open the Apex Coaching app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open the Account screen.
  4. Select Delete account permanently.
  5. Confirm the deletion prompts.

In-app deletion permanently removes your account and associated personal data from the active service.

Request Account Deletion By Email

If you cannot access the app, send an account deletion request to edwinswanith006@gmail.com. Include the email address used for your Apex Coaching account and write "Apex Coaching account deletion request" in the subject line.

Request Deletion Of Some App Data

You may also request deletion of some app data without deleting your entire account. Send a request to edwinswanith006@gmail.com and describe the data you want deleted, such as training logs, wellness logs, hydration logs, messages, or notification data.

Data Deleted

Account deletion removes account profile information and associated app data, including training logs, wellness logs, hydration logs, RPE entries, messages, notification tokens, and role-linked account data where deletion is technically and legally permitted.

Data We May Keep Temporarily

We may retain limited records when needed for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, backup restoration, or audit purposes. Backup copies are removed according to normal backup retention cycles.

Contact

For questions about account or data deletion, contact edwinswanith006@gmail.com.
