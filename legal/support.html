Frequently Asked Questions

I can't sign in. What should I do?
Double-check your email and password, and your internet connection. If you signed up with Apple or Google, use the same "Sign in with Apple" / "Sign in with Google" button — a social account and a password account with the same email are treated as one account. Still stuck? Email us and we'll help you regain access.
How do I create an athlete account?
Open the app, choose Athlete, and select "Create an account," or use Sign in with Apple / Sign in with Google on the athlete login screen. Coaches and guardians are set up by an academy — contact your coach or academy if you need one of those accounts.
How do I delete my account or my data?
Open the app, sign in, go to your Account screen, and choose "Delete account permanently." If you can't access the app, email us and we'll process the request. See our account & data deletion page for details.
Is my data private?
Yes — see our Privacy Policy for what we collect and how it's used.
The voice assistant isn't understanding me. What now?
Try again in a quiet space, or use the text-entry fallback in the same screen. If it keeps happening, tell us what you said and what it did instead so we can look into it.
My coach/guardian and I aren't linked. What do I do?
Athlete-coach and athlete-guardian links are set up by a coach inside the app. Ask your coach to add or link your account from their roster.
